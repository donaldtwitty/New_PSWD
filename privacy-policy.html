Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your information.

Last updated: May 22, 2025

Purposeful Solutions Web Design, a DBA of Twitty Enterprise LLC ("we," "us," or "our"), is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you visit our website, how we use it, and your rights regarding that information.

Information We Collect

Contact Form Submissions

When you fill out our contact form, we collect your name, email address, and the message you submit. We use this information solely to respond to your inquiry. We do not add you to any mailing list without your explicit consent.

Analytics Data

We use Google Analytics (G-S0ZR6NHZ50) to understand how visitors use our site. Google Analytics collects information such as your IP address, browser type, pages visited, time spent on pages, and referring URLs. This data is aggregated and anonymized — we cannot identify you personally from it.

Google Analytics uses cookies to collect this information. You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Cookies

Our website uses cookies for two purposes:

  • Analytics: Google Analytics places cookies to track site usage as described above.
  • Preferences: We store a small cookie to remember your display preference (light or dark mode) so it persists across visits.

You can disable cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies will not prevent the site from functioning, but your display preference will not be saved between visits.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to inquiries you submit through the contact form
  • Understand how visitors navigate our website so we can improve it
  • Remember your display preferences

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third party, ever.

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services that may process data when you visit our site:

  • Google Analytics — website traffic analysis. Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Fonts — fonts loaded from Google's CDN. Google Privacy Policy

Data Retention

Contact form submissions are retained only as long as necessary to respond to your inquiry and are not stored in any long-term database. Google Analytics data is retained according to Google's standard retention settings (26 months by default).

Your Rights (California Residents — CCPA)

If you are a California resident, the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) gives you the right to:

  • Know what personal information we have collected about you
  • Request deletion of your personal information
  • Opt out of the sale of your personal information (we do not sell personal information)
  • Non-discrimination for exercising your CCPA rights

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the information below.

Children's Privacy

Our website is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us and we will promptly delete it.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this privacy policy or how we handle your information, please contact us:

  • Website: Contact form
