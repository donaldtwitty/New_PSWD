Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about the brand identity process.

Do I receive the source files for my logo?

Yes. You own everything we create. We deliver all final files — plus the original source files in formats you can use with any designer in the future. Your brand assets belong to you, full stop.

Can you refresh my existing logo instead of starting from scratch?

Absolutely. If your current mark has equity worth preserving, a thoughtful refresh is often the smarter move. We'll assess your existing brand and recommend the approach — full redesign or evolution — that fits your situation.

How many initial concepts will I see?

We typically present 2–3 distinct initial design directions. From there, we refine the chosen direction together through as many rounds as it takes to get it right. The goal isn't quantity of concepts — it's getting to the right answer.

Does brand identity include a website?

Brand identity and web design are separate services — but they're designed to work together. Many clients start with brand identity, then move to a custom web design build using the new brand. We can plan both from the beginning so everything is cohesive from day one.
