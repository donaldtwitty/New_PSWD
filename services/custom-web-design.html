Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before we start building.

How long does a custom website take?

Most projects are completed in 4–8 weeks, depending on scope and how quickly we receive content and feedback from you. We'll give you a realistic timeline during your free consultation.

Do I need to provide my own content and photos?

We can work with whatever you have. If you have copy and images ready, we'll make them shine. If not, we'll guide you on exactly what you need and help you structure your content for maximum impact.

Can you redesign my existing website?

Absolutely. Whether you're starting from scratch or need a full redesign, our process is the same — we start with your goals, not the existing site, to ensure the new design actually moves the needle.

What happens after my site launches?

We don't disappear at launch. We offer ongoing maintenance plans to keep your site updated, secure, and performing well — and we're always available if questions come up.
