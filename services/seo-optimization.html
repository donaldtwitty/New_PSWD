Frequently Asked Questions

Straight answers to common SEO questions — no spin.

How long before I see results from SEO?

Technical fixes like page speed improvements are often visible within days. Organic ranking improvements typically take 3–6 months to show meaningful movement. We set realistic expectations at the start and keep you updated on progress along the way.

Do you guarantee a #1 Google ranking?

No — and you should be skeptical of anyone who does. Google's algorithm is not something any agency controls. What we can do is apply every proven best practice to give your site the strongest possible foundation. Most clients see meaningful improvement in visibility and qualified traffic.

What's the difference between SEO and paid ads?

Paid ads put you at the top immediately — but the traffic stops the moment you stop paying. SEO builds organic visibility that compounds over time. Both have a role; SEO is the long-term foundation that makes every other marketing channel work better.

Can you do SEO on a site you didn't build?

Yes. We can audit and optimize any website. We'll need access to your CMS or code, and we'll give you a clear picture of what we find before any work begins — no commitment required to see the audit.
