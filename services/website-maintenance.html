Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before you hand us the keys.

What exactly is included in a monthly maintenance plan?

Every plan includes content updates, regular backups, security monitoring, bug fixes, and uptime monitoring. You also get a single point of contact — no juggling multiple vendors or submitting support tickets into the void.

What if my website wasn't built by Purposeful Solutions?

No problem. We can maintain websites built by anyone, as long as we can access the platform and code. We'll do a quick review to understand the setup before work begins — no surprises.

How quickly are update requests handled?

Most routine updates are completed within 2 business days. Urgent issues — like a site going down or a critical bug — are addressed the same day.

Is there a long-term contract?

No. Our maintenance plans are month-to-month. We earn your business every month by keeping your site in great shape — not by locking you in.
